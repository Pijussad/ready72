## Q&A anchors

- **Why €473 vs the artboard €412?** The artboard did not price every durable; we use budget estimates and a shared kit. The audit target was €380–550.
- **Why 3 L per adult, not 6–12 L?** 72 hours at 1 L/day carried plus purification; 6–12 L would break every carry limit. The plan says this.
- **What if a child-only household cannot carry food?** The plan reports the gap rather than overloading a child. It never crashes.
- **AI?** The client is built, but this build does not call it; the on-device parser is what runs, and the privacy notice says so.
- **Prices?** 16 Barbora rows checked 13 Nov 2026; the rest are estimates. Decathlon blocked automation.
